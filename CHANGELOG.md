# Changelog

## 0.6.1

- Desktop flights begin after 20 seconds without interaction and return home after a 7-second trip.
- Flights travel around desktop edges and approach the center without overwriting the saved position.
- Interaction, mute, hide, and reduced motion interrupt flight and restore the avatar.
- Adventures can be disabled or previewed from the right-click menu.

## Earlier milestones

- 0.5.0: flowing plasma, orbiting particles, and smooth energy-form blending.
- 0.4.0: four-form lightning companion artwork.

The current app also includes English chat and speech, reviewed microphone transcription, saved game guides, source links, game detection, and League of Legends alerts.
